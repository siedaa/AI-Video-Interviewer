## What is broken / incomplete

- **Bluffer misranks above Average in evals.** `evals/results.md`: strong 4.6 > **bluffer 3.6** > nervous 3.4 > average 2.6 > weak 1.8. The scorer's LLM rewards fluent-but-empty answers, and the evidence guardrail checks quote *provenance*, not *correctness*. Reported, not hand-tuned (per PRD §7 exit criteria).

- **Avatar mouth-sync startup lag** — mouth stays closed for the first portion of agent speech before syncing (cosmetic, documented in `ARCHITECTURE.md`). Avatar is amplitude-driven, not phoneme/viseme-driven — the fallback tier, not the vendor path.
- **`question_plan.json` on disk has `approved_by_human: false`** — it was written by the direct prep pipeline (`run_prep.py`); the HITL-approved write (which sets `true`) is produced when you run `run_graph.py` and approve. The gate itself works and was tested.
- **Repo is private** and unlisted videos absent — must be fixed before grading, else items are zeroed.
